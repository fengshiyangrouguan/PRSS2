    """Pack pieces into stocks using first-fit with orientation and min/max counts.

    Args:
        stocks: list of dicts with 'length' and 'width' (int/float).
        pieces: list of dicts with 'length', 'width', 'min', 'max' (int/float).

        stock_types[s['length']][s['width']] = sid + 1
